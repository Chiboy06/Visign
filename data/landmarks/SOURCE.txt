Training data provenance for this local run

- nsl-photos.json: 483 MediaPipe landmark samples converted from the NSL Alphanumeric Dataset (Adeniyan, Afolayan & Johnson, 2026), Zenodo record https://zenodo.org/records/21672976, CC BY 4.0. 495 photos were downloaded; 12 were skipped because the hand detector found no hand. The language labels and hand shapes are from the source photo filenames/content. Source photos are retained locally under gitignored data/raw/nsl/.
- asl-baseline.json: 2,122 ASL landmark samples reconstructed from the repository's pre-existing public/model/letters.json so this run retains its ASL baseline. The repository attributes ASLNow! (sid220) under MIT in README.md.

Landmark model retraining command: npm run model
Landmark outputs: public/model/letters.json and public/model/poses.json
